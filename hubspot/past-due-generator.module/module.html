{# Past Due Generator — runs the n8n "Printing Statements" workflow and downloads the PDF. #}

Generate Past Due Notices

This tool builds one combined Past Due Notice PDF — a printable statement per customer — for customers who:

  • have at least one open, past‑due invoice, and
  • are scheduled to receive an order tomorrow (based on the Sales Order's Requested Delivery Date).

Customers on Route 21 (RT 21) are excluded. Statements are grouped and sorted by route, and each account prints on 2 pages (letter-free notice, double‑sided ready).

Set the two filters below, click Generate, and the PDF downloads when it’s ready. Large runs can take a few minutes — keep this page open.
